Guantánamo, tierra singular y heroica

Guantánamo, que en lengua aborigen significa «tierra entre ríos», es el más oriental y montañoso de la Isla, bañado por 600 arroyos, afluentes y ríos: Duaba, Miel, Guaso, Guantánamo, Yateras y el majestuoso Toa, el más caudaloso de Cuba. Confluyen tres climas: tropical lluvioso, tropical de sabana y una zona semidesértica en la Franja costera Sur.

Los bosques pluvios de sus serranías son de gran endemismo; en el Parque Nacional Alejandro de Humboldt, Patrimonio de la Humanidad (UNESCO), se encuentra la mayor reserva de biodiversidad y reservorio de agua potable de la cuenca del Caribe Insular.

Diego Velázquez fundó la primera Villa de Cuba: Nuestra Señora de la Asunción de Baracoa, donde se conserva «La Santa Cruz de la Parra», con la que Fray Bartolomé de las Casas oficiara la primera misa en el nuevo mundo. Entre sus exclusividades están una de las bahías de bolsa más importantes del mundo, el Yunque (Baracoa), los Monitongos (Hatibonico), las Terrazas marinas (Maisí), las Polimitas Pictas, la ranita Iberia, el único zoológico de piedras del mundo y el viaducto La Farola. Son ritmos originarios del son montuno el Nengón, el Kiribá y el Changüí.

Guantánamo, singular and heroic land

Guantánamo, which in the aboriginal language means “land between rivers”, is the easternmost and most mountainous region of the Island, washed by 600 streams, tributaries and rivers: Duaba, Miel, Guaso, Guantánamo, Yateras and the majestic Toa, Cuba's mightiest river. Three climates converge here: rainy tropical, tropical savanna, and a semi-desert zone along the Southern Coastal Belt.

The rainforests of its highlands show remarkable endemism; Alejandro de Humboldt National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, holds the largest biodiversity reserve and freshwater reservoir in the Insular Caribbean basin.

Diego Velázquez founded Cuba's first settlement, Nuestra Señora de la Asunción de Baracoa, where “La Santa Cruz de la Parra” is preserved — the cross with which Friar Bartolomé de las Casas celebrated the first mass in the New World. Local exclusives include one of the world's most important pocket bays, El Yunque (Baracoa), the Monitongos (Hatibonico), the marine terraces (Maisí), the Painted Polimitas, the Iberia frog, the world's only stone zoo and the La Farola viaduct. The Nengón, Kiribá and Changüí are homegrown rhythms of the son montuno.

Investment projects

Project 01 Agroindustry

Industrialization capacity to diversify the production of coconut horchata, grated coconut in syrup, and fruit and vegetable pulps

Proponent entity: Provincial Food Company (UEB Baracoa)

250.0 M/USD

Guantánamo is the premier coconut-producing province. The “La Primada” cannery has traditionally processed coconut derivatives, with more than 40 years of expertise, a registered trademark and sanitary registration, but faces limitations from technological obsolescence and access to financing. The project will add value to the production of coconut horchata and grated coconut in syrup.

Project 02 Agroindustry

Mini-industry for the processing of fruits and vegetables (dehydrated, pre-cooked and frozen)

Proponent entity: Guantánamo Agroindustrial Company

300.0 M/USD

A strategic national priority: increasing food production and food security while advancing toward the effective substitution of imports. Objective: build a food-preservation mini-industry with adequate technology and infrastructure, in response to the harvest peaks of quadrant 4 of the Anillo Verde project.

Project 03 Agroforestry

Organic chocolate production

Proponent entity: Agroforestry and Coconut Company of Baracoa

1,800.0 M/USD

Baracoa is the country's largest cocoa producer, with more than 1,800 t per year. The province aims to pioneer the production of fine-aroma organic Theobroma cacao L. in Cuba, guaranteeing a genuinely organic product aimed at European markets. It contributes to the sustainable development of Alejandro de Humboldt National Park and the Cuchillas del Toa Biosphere Reserve.

Project 04 Agroforestry

Technology hub for animal feed production from indigenous alternative raw materials of Baracoa

Proponent entity: Agroforestry Company of Baracoa

1,400.0 M/USD

The country's first factory of balanced dry feed using local raw materials: 300 t of dry feed monthly, 4 million liters per year of liquid silage, and 40% organic output. Feed for all species of economic interest; it strengthens exportable lines and import substitution through productive value chains.

Project 05 Forestry

Support for the timber value chain for the production and commercialization of sawn wood

3,200.0 M/USD

Comprehensive strengthening of the timber value chain in Baracoa for the production and commercialization of sawn wood for the domestic market and export, driving the sustainable use of the territory's forest potential.

Project 06 Agroforestry

Use of coconut by-products

Proponent entity: Agroforestry and Coconut Company of Baracoa

1,300.0 M/USD

An induced investment in the Guamá Coconut Oil Factory with three new lines: coconut milk, coconut water and coconut vinegar, covering 4,483 hectares of plantations. Savings in energy and fuels, ecological management of agricultural areas, and boosting of tourism, exports and import substitution.

Project 07 Carpentry

Production and commercialization of furniture and other carpentry products for the domestic market and export

Proponent entity: Carpintería Hateicito (Baracoa)

163.1 M/USD

Modernization of a carpentry workshop in Baracoa to improve its technological and production capabilities, with a production design that respects and protects the environment through the reuse and recycling of waste, creating jobs for women and young people.

Project 08 Agroindustry

Production diversification through coffee roasting and grinding

Proponent entity: Asdrúbal López Vázquez Coffee Processing Company

300.0 M/USD

Currently the province does not roast its own coffee. The project adds value with specialty coffee from the Nipe–Sagua–Baracoa massif (500–850 m above sea level, ~300 t), with distinctive flavor notes and a SCAA rating above 83 points, achieving better prices in the domestic and international markets.

Project 09 Agroforestry

“Coco Más, Baracoa”: Bottled coconut water production led by women

Proponent entity: Agroforestry and Coconut Company of Baracoa

1,518.6 M/USD

Leveraging extensive coconut plantations and a deeply rooted cultivation culture to produce bottled coconut water, offered to the local population, visitors and export markets — high revenues at minimal cost, employment for women and tourism development around the company.
